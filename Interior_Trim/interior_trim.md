# Setup and Inspection

## Tools and Materials List

- Interior Trim:
    - Drill with countersink bit
    - Screw gun with T25 drive bit
    - Finish nailer and finish nails
    - Cart full of trim boards (see material list)
    - Spacer Tiles (plastic bucket at floor level below interior trim rack)
    - Foam backer rod (3/8" and 1/2")
    - Measuring Tape
    - Pencil

- Install Vent Cap:
    - Drill with countersink bit
    - Screw gun with T25 drive bit
    - Measuring Tape
    - Pencil
    - Spirit Level
    - Support Block installation pattern
    - Vent Cap Drill pattern
    - \#10 x 3" Screws (brown)

`#pagebreak()`{=typst}

## Preparation

- Install foam backer rod in the spaces between the front and back windows and the window framing.
Use a piece of flooring or a putty knife to make insertion easier.
- Use **TRIM** nail gun and **TRIM** nails
- Nailing Pattern for ALL inside trim:
    - 2 nails at one end
    - 1 every 8"-12" in the middle
    - 2 at the other end
- Note that '2" trim' is not actually 2" wide, but is a little narrower.  Where two pieces butt up to each other, make sure that the widths match.

- **Install trim in the following order**

`#pagebreak()`{=typst}

# Baseboards

- Set down spacers against each wall to provide a small space between the baseboard and the floor, so that the flooring can "float".
These spacers are squares of thin flooring marked with an **O**, and can be found in a plastic bucket below the baseboard rack.
- Measure along a sidewall from back to front near the floor. Using long white nylon baseboards, cut a piece this length and nail in place to the side wall 
- Repeat on the other side wall.
- Measure between the side baseboards at the rear wall. Measure long (about 1/16") so it will fit snugly. Cut a length of baseboard and nail.  Save the cut-off piece for the front wall.

# Rear Window Shelf

- Check that foam backer rod is installed around the window unit
- Use pre-cut rear shelf.  Test fit the shelf into the opening, and if the gap between the rear edge of the shelf
  and the window is too large, trim the back edges of the ears on either side of the shelf to achieve a better fit.

1. Pre-drill three holes using countersink bit, as shown in the diagram, about ¾" from the rear edge of the shelf
   - Position shelf in window opening, and screw in 3 **LONG** screws

2. Cut 2x4 support block for under the shelf
   - Subtract 1 inch from shelf width ( ½" on each side )
   - Center block under the shelf with long side against the wall, **and ½" shelf overhang on each end**
   - Mark on the wall where the ends of the 2x4 should be, and then mark on the 2x4 where the lines of paneling screws fall (stud markers)
   - Preset 2 **LONG** screws in a vertical line into the block at each of the stud markers
   - Position the 2x4 up against the underside of the shelf, and lined up with the end marks on the wall
   - Drive the screws into the wall to secure the support block
   - Be sure there is no space between the wall and the support block

3. Pre-drill and insert a **LONG** screw next to the wall down through the shelf and into the 2x4 on each side of the window, about 2" in
from each edge of the shelf.

`#fullpage("Images/rear_wall.dxf", layers:"1_Paneling,3_Shelf")`{=typst}

# Rear Window Trim

Use precut pieces (3-1/8" x 35") to line the sides of the window opening

1. Nail 1 piece to each side of the window opening, edge flush with wall, and tight at the bottom. (2 nails at top, 1 every 8", 2 at the bottom)
2. Measure (very close to window) between the sides for the top of the window liner. Cut one piece of window liner that length and nail in place.
3. Measure the sides of the window from shelf to lower edge of top window liner.
    - Cut a piece of 2" trim this length for each side, and check that when these are in place, a third piece of trim will be exactly flush with the
      lower edge of the lining at the top of the window.
    - Nail 2" trim to both sides of the window, keeping the trim flush with the inside of the window lining boards.
4. Measure above the window from the outer edges of the vertical trim
    and add one inch. Cut 2" trim and nail above the window with ½" overhang on each side, keeping the trim flush with the top window lining.

# Vertical Trim (2" trim boards)

1. Back Wall corners
   - Use pieces of triangle trim (with the sharp point on one end) as a gauge.  Have one person hold it in place, and measure from
the top of the baseboard to the bottom of the pointed end of the triangle trim.
   - Cut and nail a piece of trim into each corner of the rear wall.

`#imgCtr("Images/rear_wall.dxf", layers:"1_Paneling,6_Wall_trim")`{=typst}
`#pagebreak()`{=typst}

2. Side Wall Rear Corner
   - measure from the top of the baseboards to the top of the side wall at the back corner
   - Cut and nail. Push this trim against the rear wall trim as you are nailing, to insure a tight seam. Repeat on the other side wall.

3. Sidewall Seams
   - Measure at the vertical seam between the side panels from top of baseboard to the top of the wall.
   - Cut and nail, making sure the trim is centered over the seam.
   - Repeat on the other wall.

4. Ceiling Seams
   - Using a precut piece of 2" trim 42" long, line up the bottom end of this trim with the vertical trim on the wall seam,
    and line up the other end on the ceiling panel seam near the vents.  Nail this in place.
   - Repeat on the other side of the ceiling.

`#imgCtr("Images/sidewall_trim.dxf", layers:"0_Panels,2_Vertical_Trim")`{=typst}
`#pagebreak()`{=typst}

# Top trim (2" trim boards)

1. Install the precut trim pieces with pointed ends at the top of the rear wall triangle panels.  The pointed end should be slid
    into the space between the ceiling panel and the corner trim, and then pressed against the ceiling panels while being nailed into place.
2. Install a precut piece of 2" trim 42" long on the ceiling, pressing it against the trim on the rear wall triangle panel to insure a tight fit.
3. Measure between the corner boards on the rear wall at the seam between the wall panel and the triangle panels
   -  Add 1/16", and cut a piece of 2" trim this length.
   -  Install this horizontally to cover the seam, aligning the top of the horizontal trim with the top of the vertical corner trim at each end.
4. Install a 12" piece of 2" trim vertically to cover the seam between the triangles (#3), down against the horizontal seam trim

`#imgCtr("Images/rear_wall.dxf", layers:"1_Paneling,7_triangle_trim")`{=typst}
`#pagebreak()`{=typst}

5. Measure between the side wall vertical trim boards near the top of the wall.  Add 1/16", and cut a piece of 2" trim this length.
6. Install this top trim between the vertical trim boards, aligning the top with the tops of the vertical trim at each end.
   - Repeat on the other side wall
7. Measure between the two pieces of ceiling trim near one side wall.  Add 1/16" and cut a piece of 2" trim this length
   - Install this trim between the two ceiling trim pieces, pressing it down against the top sidewall trim to insure a tight joint.
   - Repeat on the other side wall

`#imgCtr("Images/sidewall_trim.dxf",layers:"0_Panels,3_Top_Trim")`{=typst}

# Chair-rail trim
   - Measure up from the floor to the horizontal seam on each wall.  This should be 38½", but may vary.  Choose an average height, subtract 1",
  measure up from the floor this amount, and mark on the wall at this dimension.  Mark 3 places on the back wall,
  3 places on each rear side wall section, and 2 places on each side wall front section and on the front wall under the window.
  These marks will be guides to install the chair-rail (horizontal) trim.
   - Measure from back corner vertical trim on one side to the other back corner vertical trim.
   - Cut a piece of 2" trim this length plus 1/16" for a snug fit.
   - Line up back wall chair-rail trim so that its bottom edge is on the pencil marks, and nail in place
   - Measure from the back corner trim to middle vertical trim (add 1/16" so it fits snugly), and cut 2" trim to this dimension
   - Line up side wall chair-rail trim so that its bottom edge is on these marks, check that it lines up visually with the
  corresponding trim on the back wall, and nail in place.

Do NOT install chair-rail horizontal trim between vertical trim
and front wall. This will be done after Front Wall trim.

# Front Wall 

## Prepare and Inspect
   - Check that foam backer rod is installed around the window
   - `#text(16pt)[The door must be installed before the steps below.]`{=typst}

## Baseboard

1. Measure near the floor from the center of the latch-side door jamb to the sidewall baseboard.
    - Position two spacers on the floor where the baseboard will go
    - Cut a piece of baseboard, position it so that it overlaps half of the door jamb,  and nail in place
    - Do this first, to avoid hitting your head on the shelf above
2. Measure near the floor from the center of the hinge-side door jamb to the sidewall baseboard.
    - Position a spacer on the floor in this corner
    - Cut a piece of baseboard (nearly square), position it so that it overlaps half of the door jamb, and nail it in place 

`#fullpage("Images/front_wall.dxf", iwidth: 70%, layers:"1_Paneling,2_Baseboards")`{=typst}

# Shelf and Window
1. Install Shelf
    - Use pre-cut front shelf.
    - Test fit the shelf
    - If the extension does not touch the wall, trim the edge that sits in the window frame
    - Otherwise, if the gap between the window and the edge of the shelf is too big, trim the extension (jigsaw)
2. Pre-drill and countersink 3 places at shelf edge near the window.
    - Position shelf in window frame, and screw in with 3 **LONG** screws near bottom of the window.
3. Cut 2x4 shelf support block, subtracting 1/2" from shelf width
    - Place 2x4 under the shelf with 4 inch side against the wall
    - This block **must be** flush with door side of the shelf
    - There will be 1/2" shelf overhang on the side wall side
    - Mark on the 2x4 where the line of paneling screw falls (stud marker).
    - Mark about 2" in from each end and 3/4" from the top of the 2x4
    - Preset **LONG** screws into the block as shown in the diagram
    - Position the block up against the bottom of the shelf
    - Drive the screws into the wall
    - Be sure that the 2x4 is tight against the wall.  If not, back the screw out and re-install it, putting pressure on the 2x4
4. Pre-drill and install 3 **LONG** screws next to the wall going through the shelf and into the 2X4 on both sides of the window as shown in diagram
5. Nail precut pieces of window liner (3-1/8 x 35) to the side walls of the front window opening
    - Be sure that the front edge of these pieces is flush with the interior of the wall panel
    - Place the precut piece of window liner tight against the shelf, leaving any gap at the top
6.  Measure (very close to window) between the sides for the top of the window piece. Cut one piece of window liner that length and nail in place

`#fullpage("Images/front_wall.dxf", iwidth: 75%, layers:"1_Paneling,3_Shelf,4_window_liner")`{=typst}

# Front Wall Trim

   - Measure horizontally from the inner edge of the window opening to the center of the door jamb next to the window ( \~5½ inches)
   - Rip a 9½" x 96" trim board to this width on the table saw; save the wider piece (\~5½ inches) for step 5
1. Measure the height on the side wall behind the door from the baseboard to the top of the wall
   - Cut a piece of 2" trim this length, and install it on the side wall.
   - Check that the narrow piece from the ripped board will span from the center of the door jamb to within ¼" of the side wall trim.
  If it is too narrow, stop and ask for help from the leadership.
2. Use pieces of triangle trim (with the sharp point on one end) as a gauge.  Behind the door, have one person hold it in place, and measure from
the top of the baseboard to the bottom of the pointed end of the triangle trim.
   - Cut the narrow piece from step 1 to length, and nail in place, halfway across the door jamb
3. Similarly, on the window-side corner, measure from the baseboard to the bottom of the pointed end of the triangle trim
   - Cut a piece of 2" trim this length, and install on the front wall, pressing it into the corner
4. On the side wall, measure from the baseboard to the top of the wall.  Cut and install a piece of 2" trim,
   pressing it tight against the front-wall corner trim. 
5. Measure between these two corner boards on the front wall near the horizontal seam between the wall panel and the triangles
   - Add 1/16", and cut a piece of 2" trim to this length
   - Install this trim between the corner boards, lined up with the tops of these corner boards
6. Install triangle trim on both sides of the front wall, pressing it up against the ceiling panels
   - Install a pre-cut 42" length of 2" trim on each ceiling panel, pressing it up against the front-wall triangle trim to insure a tight fit
   - Position a 12" piece of 2" trim vertically over the center seam in the front triangle and nail.
7. Measure from the top horizontal trim on the front wall to the baseboard and cut the wide trim from step 1 to this length
   - Mark where a cutout must be made on this wide trim for the front shelf and support block
   - Cut out a notch for the shelf using a jigsaw. Nail in place, being sure that the edge next to the window is flush with the window liner.

`#fullpage("Images/front_wall.dxf", iwidth: 75%, layers:"1_Paneling,5_trim")`{=typst}

# Final Trim

1. Measure above the door from the sidewall trim to the 5½" board. Cut 2" trim and nail in place over the door,
  with the bottom edge halfway over the door frame.
2. Measure, cut, and install 2" trim on the top and wall-side of the front window flush with window opening liner
     - top trim has ½" overhang on the right side
3. Measure, cut, and install 2" chair-rail trim on the side wall in the corner next to the shelf end.  Line this piece up using the pencil marks 
4. Measure, cut, and install 2" chair-rail trim on the front wall under the window
5. Measure, cut, and install 2" top trim on the side walls between the corner trim and the mid-wall vertical trim
6. Measure close to the wall and between the two ceiling trim boards at the front of the home
   - Add 1/16" and cut a piece of 2" trim this length.
   - Install this trim between the ceiling trim boards, pressing it down against the wall trim to insure a tight joint
7. DON'T FORGET - Measure, cut, and nail in place a small piece of trim on the front wall behind the door, to match the middle trim on the side wall.
